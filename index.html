Gunu bolu... ya kesar? Honestly, tumhare itne names hain ki kabhi kabhi samajh hi nahi aata ki kis naam se bulaun.

Sabse pehle tumhari eyes ki baat karte hain. Yaar, tumhari eyes bilkul baby jaisi innocent aur cute hain. Unme ek alag si softness hai. Kabhi unhe dekho toh lagta hai ki tum kuch bole bina bhi bahut kuch keh sakti ho.

Aur phir tumhare cheeks... itne soft hain ki unke baare mein mention karna toh banta tha. Aur jab tum smile karti ho aur tumhare cheeks par woh cute se dimples aate hain, honestly woh moment aur bhi special ho jaata hai.

Tumhare hairstyle ka bhi apna ek alag charm hai. Tum apne hair kisi bhi style mein rakho, woh tumhari personality ka part lagta hai. Aur shayad isi wajah se tumhari har look mein kuch na kuch unique hota hai.

Aur tumhari voice... yaar, seriously, tumhari voice bahut achhi hai. Kabhi normal baat karte hue, kabhi call par masti karte hue, aur kabhi bina kisi reason ke random baatein karte hue, tumhari awaaz har moment ko aur interesting bana deti hai.

Calls par tumhari masti bhi alag level ki hoti hai. Kabhi hasi-mazaak, kabhi random topics, kabhi serious baatein aur kabhi bina kisi reason ke conversation ko interesting bana dena. Shayad isi wajah se tumse baat karna kabhi boring nahi lagta.

Aur haan, tumhara gussa... woh bhi kaafi famous hai. Kabhi kabhi tum itni jaldi naraz ho jaati ho ki samajhne mein thoda time lagta hai ki exactly galti hui kya hai.

Especially jab main kisi doosri ladki se baat karta hoon aur tum naraz ho jaati ho. Tum chahe kuch bhi bolo, lekin kabhi kabhi tumhari jealousy aur tumhara possessive hona clearly notice ho jaata hai.

Phir bhi, ye sab tumhe tum banata hai. Tumhari cute eyes, tumhare soft cheeks, tumhare dimples, tumhara hairstyle, tumhari amazing voice, calls par tumhari masti, tumhara gussa aur tumhara naraz hona... sab milkar ek hi person ko describe karte hain: Vishakha.

Ya Gunu.

Naam teen ho sakte hain, lekin tumhari personality sirf ek hai, aur honestly, uska duplicate milna mushkil hai.

Bas aise hi rehna. Thodi funny, thodi different, thodi stubborn, thodi naraz hone wali, aur bilkul apne jaisi.

PLEASE GUNU, RAAT KO JALDI MAT SOYA KARO.
